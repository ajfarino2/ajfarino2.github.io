Before you press send

Do they need the app?

No. A Smart Link opens in their browser so they can choose a music service. They don’t need TuneTransit to listen.

Which music services?

Apple Music, Spotify, TIDAL, and YouTube Music. Availability of a song can vary between catalogs.

What does it cost?

Free includes 3 song/album searches per day and new mixtapes with up to 5 songs. Premium adds unlimited searches, mixtape designs, Story Cards, and more. Compare Free and Premium →

Are these the actual app screens?

Yes. These are native renders from TuneTransit 3.0, using example songs and activity. The Story Cards are exported directly from the app.

The small print

Different apps.
Same good music.

TuneTransit connects songs across Apple Music, Spotify, TIDAL, and YouTube Music. Find a song, share a Smart Link, or put together a mixtape for someone.

Does my friend need TuneTransit?

They can open your Smart Link in a browser and choose their music service without installing the app.

Where can I use it?

On iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Vision Pro. Smart Links open on other devices too.

Is it free?

TuneTransit is free to download, with optional Premium purchases. See current features and prices in the app.

What am I seeing here?

The screens come directly from TuneTransit’s SwiftUI app. Songs, artwork, and sharing activity are examples. Story Cards are exports from the app.
