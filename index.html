Before you board

Does my friend need TuneTransit?

No. Your link opens in their browser, on iPhone, Android, or a computer. They tap the music app they use and the song is there. No download, no account.

Which music apps does it work with?

Apple Music, Spotify, TIDAL, and YouTube Music, in any direction. Catalogs differ a little between services, so a song can occasionally be missing from one of them.

Will they hear the same version I sent?

Yes. TuneTransit keeps the exact recording you shared instead of switching to a remix, a live take, or a “feat.” version on the other service.

What does it cost?

TuneTransit is free to download, with 3 song or album searches a day and mixtapes of up to 5 songs. Premium adds unlimited searches, bigger mixtapes, Story Cards, and more for $1.99 a month or $9.99 once.

Where can I use it?

On iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Vision Pro. You can also ask Siri to find a song, and Premium adds TuneMessage for sending songs right inside Messages.

Who makes TuneTransit?

One developer. Questions, ideas, and bug reports go straight to the person who built it at mail@tunetransit.app.
