Privacy policy

TuneTransit 3.1 · Effective November 1, 2026

This policy explains how Anthony Farino (“we,” “us”) handles information in TuneTransit, its extensions and widgets, and related services, including core.tunetransit.app and share.tunetransit.app.

Searching for music, creating a public link, and connecting a listening service involve different data. Here is what happens when you use each.

1. Information we use

Music searches and links

We process the song or artist you search for, music URLs you paste or share into the app, and results such as titles, artists, artwork, previews, and streaming-service links. Requests may pass through TuneTransit’s backend and music catalog providers. Matching results may be cached to improve future lookups.

The app stores search history and preferences on your device. App-generated device and creator identifiers, authentication credentials, app version, and usage counters help connect your content to your installation, protect requests, and apply free-use limits. Some usage information is stored through Apple’s iCloud/CloudKit services.

Smart Links, Tracks, replies, and mixtapes

When you create a Smart Link, the backend stores its music metadata, destination links, creator identifier, creation time, and any connection to a song you are replying to. Tracks uses sent and received links, opening times, and reply relationships to show Arrivals and Departures.

Mixtapes store the title, songs and their order, optional personal note and creator name, design choices, and contributor information you submit. This information is stored on our servers so a shared mixtape can be opened and updated.

Shared-link activity can include the time a link is opened, the music service selected, approximate country or region, a broad referrer category, and a device-related identifier or hash. We use it for link activity, aggregate music discovery, service operation, and abuse prevention. We also keep aggregate counts of shared-page views and taps (such as a music service, Open in TuneTransit, or the App Store), with device type, country, and broad referrer category, and of steps completed in the app, such as a first send. These counts are not stored with a device identifier or your account, and we use them to understand how people find and start using TuneTransit. Hosting infrastructure also processes network information, such as IP addresses, needed to deliver and protect requests.

Optional Now Playing and Recently Played

If you enable Apple Music access, TuneTransit can read authorized music and playback information. Optional background updates can send a music user token, device or notification identifiers, and playback metadata to the TuneTransit backend. Recently played information may be stored locally and synced through iCloud; Apple Music history snapshots use a private CloudKit database.

If you connect Last.fm for Spotify or TIDAL detection, TuneTransit uses your Last.fm username to request listening metadata. The backend may store that username, notification identifiers, registration information, and queued listening events to deliver Now Playing and history updates. TuneTransit does not ask for your Last.fm password. Disconnecting the integration stops its future updates; see the deletion section for stored information.

Microphone, clipboard, and photos

Live Capture: When you start recognition and allow microphone access, Apple’s ShazamKit processes audio to identify a song. TuneTransit uses the resulting music metadata. The app does not upload a raw microphone recording to the TuneTransit backend or save it as a recording.

Clipboard: The app can detect or suggest a copied music link. When you choose to use a link, its URL is processed as a music search. The clipboard is not uploaded as a general history.

Story Cards: Cards are rendered in the app from song metadata and artwork. Saving or sharing a card sends the resulting image to the destination you choose. Photo-library access is requested when needed to save an export.

Premium, notifications, and support

Apple processes Premium purchases. StoreKit provides transaction and entitlement information to enable paid features; we do not receive your card or bank details. If notifications are enabled, device notification tokens and related routing information support delivery through Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging.

If you contact us, we receive the information you include, such as an email address, description of an issue, and any links or attachments you send.

2. What other people can see

A Smart Link or mixtape is shareable by URL. Anyone with that URL may view and forward its contents. For a mixtape, this includes the personal note, creator name, songs, and contributor details you choose to include. Do not add information you want to keep confidential.

A link creator can see activity and replies associated with their shared music. Aggregate charts and recent sharing activity can display music metadata, event times, selected services, and approximate geographic information. These features are based on sharing and link events; they are not the retired friends’ Social Feed or TuneTransit Station.

When you export a Story Card or open music in another service, that destination’s own privacy practices apply. Copies saved or forwarded by other people are outside our control.

3. Why we process information, and with whom

We use information to find and match music, create and deliver shared content, maintain your history and replies, provide optional listening updates, verify Premium access, respond to support requests, and protect the service.

  • Apple: music access and recognition, iCloud/CloudKit, purchases, device permissions, and notification delivery.
  • Music and metadata services: Apple Music, Spotify, TIDAL, YouTube/Google, Last.fm, and, where used for matching, MusicBrainz/ListenBrainz and Cover Art Archive. Requests can include search terms, music identifiers, links, or an authorized account identifier.
  • Hosting and delivery providers: infrastructure hosting TuneTransit’s backend and shared links, including Cloudflare, and Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications.
  • People you share with: the information described above, through the link or export you choose to share.

We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Information may also be disclosed when legally required or necessary to address fraud, abuse, or threats to users or the service.

Where applicable, our grounds for processing include providing features you request, legitimate interests in service reliability and security, consent for optional permissions and integrations, and legal obligations. Data may be processed in countries where our providers operate, under applicable data-protection requirements.

4. Storage, retention, and deletion

Different kinds of information have different lifetimes:

  • Local history and settings remain until cleared or removed. Some preferences or credentials may also be in shared app storage, Keychain, device backups, or iCloud; uninstalling the app does not necessarily remove those copies.
  • Shared links, mixtapes, and activity records are stored to keep shared content and its history available. They do not automatically disappear when you uninstall TuneTransit. Delete a mixtape through its available app controls, or contact us to request removal of a link or associated server records.
  • Listening integrations retain registration data while connected. Backend listening events can be queued until delivered and acknowledged. Unregistering an integration removes its registration and associated queued events; existing local history, cloud copies, or content you already shared may remain.
  • Operational records and support messages are retained as needed to resolve requests, operate and secure the service, and meet legal obligations. We do not promise a single automatic deletion period for every data category.

Use mail@tunetransit.app for server-data access or deletion requests. Include the relevant shared URLs and enough context for us to locate your data. Do not send passwords or authentication tokens. We may need to verify that a request concerns your content before acting.

Removing a feature from version 3.0 does not itself delete records created by an older version. You may also request deletion of legacy Station or Social Feed information. Removal cannot recall copies already exported or saved by recipients; records required for legal or security purposes may need to be retained.

We use measures such as authenticated requests, encrypted transport, and platform storage protections. No system can guarantee absolute security.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Allow or revoke microphone, music, photo, and notification permissions in device settings.
  • Disconnect optional listening integrations in TuneTransit, and manage music-service permissions with the provider.
  • Choose what to put in a public link, mixtape, note, or Story Card. Ask for removal of shared content you control.
  • Manage Premium subscriptions through your Apple account. Deleting app data does not cancel a subscription.
  • Contact us about access, correction, deletion, or a copy of information associated with you.

Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, request portability, or complain to a data-protection authority. California residents may have rights to know, correct, and delete personal information, and to exercise applicable privacy rights without unlawful discrimination. We assess requests under the laws that apply to them.

6. This website

The website’s app previews use example music and activity; viewing them does not search your library or send songs. The hosting and sign-in provider may process technical and account information to deliver this site and enforce its access settings. Shared music links have the activity processing described above.
