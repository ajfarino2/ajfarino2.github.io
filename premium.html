Premium

Most of TuneTransit is free. Premium is the rest.

Sharing links, Tracks, Station Board and mixtapes of up to 5 songs cost nothing, and opening a link is always free. Premium takes off the limits and adds Story Cards, mixtape designs and Now Playing widgets.

MONTHLY$1.99a month. Cancel anytime.

LIFETIME$9.99once. Premium for good.

Either one gets you everything in the Premium column. You upgrade inside the app. Prices are in US dollars, and the app shows your local price.

Story Cards

Turn any song into a full-size image for Instagram Stories, or anywhere else you post. Premium includes all five designs.

What else changes

No daily search limit

Free includes 3 song or album searches a day, and that one allowance covers the app, TuneMessage, Siri and the share sheet. Premium removes the limit in all four.

Bigger mixtapes, in your design

A free tape holds 5 songs and uses the classic cassette. With Premium a tape holds up to 500, and you choose its colors, case, label and stickers. Notes are included either way.

Now Playing widgets

Widgets put your latest song on the Home Screen or Lock Screen of a supported iPhone or iPad.

Buying and restoring

Where do I upgrade?

Inside the app, on its Premium page. TuneTransit itself is free to download.

How do Monthly and Lifetime differ?

Only in how you pay. Monthly is $1.99 a month and Lifetime is $9.99 once. Both include every Premium feature.

Can I cancel Monthly?

Yes. It’s a recurring subscription that you manage or cancel through your Apple account.

Already have Monthly?

Buying Lifetime does not cancel Monthly. Cancel the monthly subscription in the App Store after upgrading to avoid further monthly charges.

Already bought Premium?

Use Restore purchases on the app’s Premium page with the Apple account you used to buy it.

Does my friend need Premium?

No. Opening a Smart Link or a mixtape is free in any browser, with nothing to download.
