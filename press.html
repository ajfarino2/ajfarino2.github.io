Why it exists

My friends are split across Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube Music. Every song we sent each other turned into the same routine: “what’s this called?”, a search, and often the wrong version. TuneTransit started as a fix for that one small, constant annoyance.

It’s been on the App Store since June 2025, and it’s still one person: the design, the code, this website and the inbox.

Descriptions you can copy

Use them as they are or rewrite them. Either is fine with me.

  • One line

  • Short

    TuneTransit is a music-sharing app for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Vision Pro and iPhone Duo. Search for a song or paste a link, and it makes one Smart Link that opens in Apple Music, Spotify, TIDAL or YouTube Music. The person you send it to doesn’t need the app or an account, and you can see when they open it.

  • Long

    160 words

    TuneTransit solves music’s version of the green-bubble problem: sending a song to someone who uses a different streaming service. Search for a song, paste a link from another app, or let Live Capture identify what’s playing nearby, and TuneTransit makes one Smart Link that opens in Apple Music, Spotify, TIDAL or YouTube Music. Friends don’t need the app to listen, and they can send a song back from the link’s page.

    Tracks keeps every song you send and receive, with a receipt showing how many times each link was opened and in which app. Mixtapes bundle songs with a personal note, Story Cards turn a song into an Instagram Story, and the Station Board shows what TuneTransit listeners are sharing this week. It also works from Siri, Shortcuts, Messages and the share sheet.

    TuneTransit runs on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Vision Pro and iPhone Duo. It’s free, with an optional Premium upgrade, and is made by independent developer Anthony Farino.

Stops worth writing about

  1. Their link, your app

    Paste a link from any of the four services, search by name, or let Live Capture name what’s playing nearby. Matching keeps the exact recording you meant, not the remix or the live take, and part of it runs on device with Core ML.

  2. Know when they listen

    Every link you send keeps a receipt, like “3 opens · 2 Spotify, 1 Apple Music”. You get a notification the first time someone opens it, and they can send a song back from the link’s page without installing anything.

  3. Mixtapes, with a note

    Name the tape, say why each song made the cut, split it into Side A and Side B, and send it as one link. Free mixtapes hold 5 songs, and Premium mixtapes hold up to 500.

  4. The Station Board

    The songs shared by the most people on TuneTransit this week, and a running list of shared links as they get opened.

  5. Made for iPhone Duo

    Folded, every screen uses the whole cover display around its vertical status bar. Open, Search, Mixtapes and Tracks spread across the inner display.

  6. On Apple Vision Pro

    The tab bar becomes a small subway map floating beside the window: five colored stations on a rail, with Search in the middle. TuneTransit has been on Vision Pro since February 2026.

Also in the app: Story Cards in five designs, a Messages app, Siri and Shortcuts actions, and Home and Lock Screen widgets.
