ABOUT THE APP

Share music across streaming services.

  • One link for every music app.

    Search for a song or album, or paste a link from another service. TuneTransit makes a Smart Link that opens in Apple Music, Spotify, TIDAL or YouTube Music, so friends on Android and the web can open it too.

  • Mixtapes.

    Gather a few songs, add a personal note and send the whole mixtape as one link.

  • Story Cards.

    Turn a song into a shareable cover in one of five designs, ready for Instagram Stories and other apps.

  • Siri and Shortcuts.

    Ask Siri to find a song and get a result card with Open and Copy Link actions.

  • Messages.

    TuneMessage, the app for Messages, lets you search and send songs without leaving a conversation.

  • Everywhere you listen.

    Built for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Vision Pro, with layouts designed for both iPhone Duo displays.

WHAT’S NEW IN 3.1

Made for iPhone Duo.

  1. Folded and open layouts.

    Folded, every screen uses the full width of the cover display around its vertical status bar. Open, Search, Mixtapes, Story Cards and the Station Board spread across the inner display.

  2. Siri and Apple Intelligence search.

    Find songs with Siri and Apple Intelligence through App Intents, with a redesigned Shortcuts result card that has Open and Copy Link actions.

  3. TuneMessage, in light and dark.

    The Messages app now follows light and dark mode, with a gradient that matches TuneTransit’s 3.0 colors.

  4. Exact-recording matching.

    Links keep the exact recording you shared instead of switching to a remix or a “feat.” version on another service.
